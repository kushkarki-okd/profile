About Me

A quick read on who I am and what I build.

I'm an energetic, problem-solving backend developer based in Dhulikhel, working primarily in Python and Django, with a growing focus on building SaaS solutions from the ground up. I like turning ideas into working products clean APIs, solid architecture, and features that actually solve the problem they were built for.

Alongside development, I create tech-focused YouTube videos, breaking down concepts and sharing what I learn along the way. I'm currently exploring the DevOps side of things as well tools like AWS and Docker so I can take what I build all the way from code to a running, reliable deployment.

I also have a strong curiosity for data science and machine learning, and I enjoy exploring how they can be applied beyond theory into real products. Whatever I take on, I always give it my best.

Longer term, I carry a dream of building my own startup right here in Nepal creating something meaningful, from the ground up, with a team that believes in solving real problems.
