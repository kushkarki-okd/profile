About Me

A quick read on who I am and what I build.

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack developer based in Kathmandu, working mostly in Python and Django, with a growing focus on cloud infrastructure and systems design. I like building things that stay up at 3am — clean APIs, sane deployments, and services that fail gracefully.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of application development and DevOps: shipping Django services, then wiring the AWS side — EC2, S3, IAM, load balancers — so the thing I built actually survives production traffic.

I'm also curious about AI/ML and where it fits into everyday product work, not just research demos. Outside of shipping code, I enjoy breaking down technical concepts for other people — writing docs, mentoring juniors, and occasionally speaking at local meetups.

Longer term, I'm drawn to the startup side of things: small teams, fast iteration, and owning a product end-to-end — from the database schema to the deploy pipeline.
